# Research Question Guidance

- Question notes capture uncertainties, unresolved observations, research directions, and explored or abandoned paths. Record observations, their sources, and potential relevance separately from possible explanations.
- During initial ideation or when progress stalls, examine whether the current assumptions, objectives, or unit of analysis limit the research question. Preserve credible alternative formulations until evidence supports a choice.
- Investigate, defer, or dismiss unresolved observations and alternative questions with a brief rationale. Record what new evidence would justify revisiting deferred items.
- Each active question should identify the next evidence needed or link to the experiment that can resolve it; an unresolved observation need not have a hypothesis yet.
- Closed questions should contain or link to the answering claim, decision, or experiment conclusion. Link durable research choices to `../decisions/` rather than duplicating their rationale.
- Name question files `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` using the date the question is recorded.
- Track question files from the category dashboard as `[question](YYYY-MM-DD-question-title.md): Pending`, then replace `Pending` with the answering claim, deferring decision, cancelling decision, or linked experiment conclusion.
